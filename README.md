# AI Customer Risk & Engagement

An AI-powered customer risk intelligence and engagement platform.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing customer behaviour, identifying
customer risk, prioritizing collection actions, and supporting
intelligent customer engagement.

## Project Phases

### Phase 1 — Payment Risk & Collection Prioritization

Analysis of customer payment behaviour, risk classification,
collection prioritization, and explainable recommendations.

### Phase 2 — AI Call Agent

Development of an AI-powered customer engagement and calling workflow.

### Phase 3 — Multi-Source Customer Risk Intelligence

Integration of additional customer-related signals to improve risk
intelligence and decision support.
