SLT — Payment Reliability / Creditworthiness Proxy (PROTOTYPE)
==============================================================
Source: Step 13 classification features + invoice totals from Billing
Not a bank/credit-bureau score or future-default probability.
Not authorized for autonomous customer decisions.

Scoring (illustrative weights, out of 100):
  25 points: settled bills / total bills
  20 points: payment timing consistency
  15 points: positive average payment delay, capped at 30 days
  25 points: current overdue days (90-day cap) and unpaid past-due bills (3-bill cap)
  10 points: outstanding balance relative to average monthly invoice (3x cap)
   5 points: collection actions (12 cap) and suspensions (2 cap)

Proposed interpretation: 80-100 Strong; 60-79.9 Moderate; 40-59.9 Low; below 40 Very low.
Fewer than 3 settled bills: no score; manual review.
All weights, cutoffs, definitions and time horizons require SLT business approval.
The input workbook contains synthetic records; do not infer real creditworthiness.
Source reference class labels and customer type are NOT scoring features.
This result should complement, not replace, customer behaviour and human review.
